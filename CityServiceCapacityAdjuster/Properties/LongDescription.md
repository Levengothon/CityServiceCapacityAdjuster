This mod allows you to adjust various City Service Capacity options. Currently, it supports the following features:

# City Service Options

- **Post Office**
	- Adjust Sorting Speed (Base rate of 50k; can be increased up to 5x.)
- **Cargo Terminal Station** 
	- Adjust Truck Capacity (Base of 16 trucks; can be increased up to 5x.)
- **School** 
	- Adjust Capacity (Can be increased up to 5x.)
- **Crematorium** 
	- Adjust Burn Speed (Can be increased up to 5x.)
	- Adjust Hearse Multiplier (Can be increased up to 5x.)
- **Transport Depot** 
	- Adjust Depot Capacity
	- Adjust Public Transport Vehicle Capacity
- **Garbage**
	- Adjust Truck Capacity

# Traffic Adjuster

Additionally, the mod allows you to mitigate some unsafe traffic behaviors, including:

- Reducing unsafe pedestrian crossings.
- Reducing unsafe car behaviors (various behaviors).
	- Allow unsafe behavior
	- Vanilla behaviour
	- Low reduced unsafe behavior
	- High reduced unsafe behavior

To observe changes in traffic behavior, you will need to wait for the simulation system to adjust to these new settings.
